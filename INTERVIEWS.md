# Asynchronous Technical Decision Research

Fractal Chat is researching how technical teams make high-cost, multi-option decisions and turn scattered investigation into records that other people can review.

**[Complete the asynchronous questionnaire](https://github.com/LeoLiao0806Xuan/Fractal_Chat/issues/new?template=user_research.yml)**

The questionnaire is completed independently on GitHub and usually takes 10–15 minutes. No meeting, call, recording, or real-time interview is required.

## Who we want to speak with

Job title is not the entry criterion. You may be a good participant if, in the last 30 days, you were involved in a technical decision that:

- compared two or more viable alternatives;
- required documentation, review, or explanation to someone else;
- carried meaningful delivery, cost, security, operational, or architectural risk;
- involved fragmented sources, conflicting AI answers, repeated analysis, or rework.

Software engineers, tech leads, architects, engineering managers, independent developers, maintainers, founders, product managers, consultants, and platform, data, security, or operations specialists are all welcome when the task fits.

## What the questionnaire involves

- **Length:** approximately 10–15 minutes;
- **Format:** a written, asynchronous account of one recent real decision and the workflow around it;
- **Topics:** trigger, alternatives, evidence, tools, collaboration, review, rework, outcome, and what happened afterward;
- **Optional follow-up:** public, asynchronous questions in the submitted GitHub issue or a separate trial using a sanitized decision.

This is product research, not a job interview, sales call, performance assessment, or request for professional advice. There is no requirement to attend a meeting or use Fractal Chat.

## Privacy and research boundaries

- Do not share API keys, customer data, source code, internal URLs, security-sensitive details, or employer-confidential information.
- Generalize names and values while preserving the structure of the decision.
- The asynchronous questionnaire does not collect audio or video.
- Research notes should minimize identifying details and be used to evaluate product assumptions and workflows.
- Public findings should be aggregated or sanitized unless a participant separately approves a specific attribution or example.
- Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question or stop the session at any time.

## How to participate

Open the [asynchronous research questionnaire](https://github.com/LeoLiao0806Xuan/Fractal_Chat/issues/new?template=user_research.yml) and share a sanitized recent technical decision.

The form asks about:

1. the type and cost of the decision;
2. alternatives, tools, evidence, disagreement, review, and rework;
3. what would make the result understandable six months later; and
4. which Fractal Chat capabilities might help or add unnecessary work.

The submitted issue is public. Do not include contact details or identifying information. If you allow follow-up questions, watch the issue for a maintainer reply and continue asynchronously.

## What we are trying to learn

The research is intended to test, not assume, that Fractal Chat can help users:

- preserve multiple independent alternatives and dissenting views;
- distinguish evidence from model-generated opinion;
- reduce manual synthesis and avoidable rework;
- create a draft that can be reviewed without being rewritten elsewhere;
- return with a second real decision after the first task is complete.

Positive comments alone are not treated as validation. Repeat use, external review, real artifacts, workflow change, and concrete pilot commitments are stronger evidence.
